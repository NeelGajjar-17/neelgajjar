WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The interface changes.
The reasoning stays consistent.

01

Context before action

Study and participant state determine whether a record is actually unexpected.

02

Rules surface, people decide

Edit checks and QC narrow attention without pretending to know the source truth.

03

Preserve competing evidence

Reconciliation keeps both source values visible until the discrepancy is understood.

04

Readiness is evidence based

A percentage can summarize status, but open required activities still control the lock decision.

WHY THIS LAB EXISTS

A portfolio should show how someone thinks.

This demonstration is intentionally small. It shows the review sequence, the distinction between automation and judgment, and the importance of traceable decisions without exposing confidential clinical trial information.
