04 / WHAT CHANGED

The work became more connected.

With greater responsibility, the key question shifted from “is my task complete?” to “is the study data pathway controlled enough for the next decision?”

THENComplete assigned review→NOW

Prioritize what needs review and why.

THENTest configured behavior→NOW

Connect protocol intent, design choices, UAT and production behavior.

THENIdentify discrepancies→NOW

Coordinate context, resolution and documented reconciliation status.

THENSupport closeout→NOW

Drive the evidence and dependencies behind database lock readiness.
